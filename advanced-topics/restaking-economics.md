# Restaking Economics

Restaking is a mechanism that allows Ethereum validators to reuse their staked ETH to secure additional protocols.

This concept has gained attention through protocols such as EigenLayer.

---

# Traditional Staking

In Ethereum proof-of-stake:

validators stake ETH  
validators validate blocks  
validators receive staking rewards

The staked ETH secures only the Ethereum network.

---

# Restaking Concept

Restaking allows the same capital to secure multiple services.

Examples of services include:

- data availability layers
- oracle networks
- cross-chain bridges
- middleware protocols

Validators opt-in to secure these additional systems.

---

# Economic Model

Restaking introduces additional revenue sources.

Validators may earn:

- base staking rewards
- restaking rewards
- service-specific incentives

This increases capital efficiency for staked ETH.

---

# Risks

Restaking introduces new risks.

Slashing risk

Validators may be penalized by multiple systems.

Systemic risk

Failures in external protocols may impact Ethereum validators.

Complex incentive structures

Participants must evaluate multiple risk layers.

---

# Future Implications

Restaking could transform Ethereum into a security layer for many blockchain services.

This concept is sometimes described as "cryptoeconomic security as a service".

---

# Conclusion

Restaking represents a new frontier in blockchain economic design.

However, balancing capital efficiency with systemic risk remains an open research problem.
