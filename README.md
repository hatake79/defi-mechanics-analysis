# DeFi Mechanism Lab


A research repository exploring the **core mechanisms, economic models, and market structure of decentralized finance (DeFi).**

This repository breaks down how modern DeFi protocols work — from **automated market makers and lending systems to MEV markets and Ethereum infrastructure.**

The goal is to analyze DeFi **from first principles** and understand the incentives, risks, and architecture that power decentralized financial systems.

---

# What This Repository Covers

This research lab explores several major areas of the DeFi ecosystem.

## Core DeFi Mechanisms

Fundamental building blocks of decentralized finance:

* Automated Market Makers (AMM)
* Liquidity pool mechanics
* Lending and borrowing systems
* Liquidation engines
* Interest rate models

These mechanisms form the backbone of most DeFi protocols.

---

## MEV and Blockspace Markets

Transaction ordering in blockchains creates new forms of economic activity.

This repository studies:

* MEV extraction strategies
* Sandwich attacks
* Arbitrage systems
* Liquidation bots
* MEV supply chain

Understanding MEV is essential for analyzing modern blockchain market structure.

---

## DeFi Market Structure

As DeFi evolves, new trading architectures are emerging.

Topics include:

* Liquidity fragmentation
* Order flow auctions
* Intent-based trading
* Batch auction systems

These systems attempt to improve **execution quality, fairness, and capital efficiency.**

---

## Tokenomics and Incentive Design

Protocols rely on incentive systems to attract liquidity and users.

Research includes:

* Liquidity mining programs
* Mercenary capital dynamics
* Protocol-owned liquidity
* veToken governance models

Understanding token incentives is critical for sustainable protocol growth.

---

## Risk and Security

DeFi systems face unique risks.

This repository analyzes:

* Oracle manipulation attacks
* Flash loan exploits
* Cascading liquidations
* Stablecoin depegs

Risk analysis helps improve **protocol resilience and system stability.**
