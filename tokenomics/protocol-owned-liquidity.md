# Protocol-Owned Liquidity (POL)

Protocol-owned liquidity refers to liquidity that is owned and controlled directly by the protocol rather than external liquidity providers.

This model emerged as a response to the instability caused by mercenary liquidity.

---

# Traditional Liquidity Model

In early DeFi protocols, liquidity was primarily provided by external users.

Protocols incentivized liquidity through token rewards.

However, this model had a major weakness:

liquidity could leave the protocol at any time.

---

# POL Concept

Protocol-owned liquidity aims to solve this problem.

Instead of renting liquidity from users, the protocol accumulates its own liquidity.

This liquidity remains permanently within the ecosystem.

---

# How Protocols Acquire Liquidity

Protocols can accumulate liquidity through several mechanisms.

Examples include:

- token bond sales
- treasury accumulation
- liquidity reserve funds

These mechanisms allow the protocol to control its own market liquidity.

---

# Advantages

Protocol-owned liquidity provides several benefits:

- stable trading conditions
- reduced dependence on liquidity mining
- long-term market depth

It also allows protocols to capture trading fees directly.

---

# Challenges

POL systems require careful treasury management.

Risks include:

- treasury mismanagement
- token price volatility
- inefficient capital allocation

---

# Conclusion

Protocol-owned liquidity represents an alternative model for sustainable liquidity management in DeFi ecosystems.
