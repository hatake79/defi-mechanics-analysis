# veToken Model

The vote-escrow token model (veToken) is a governance and incentive mechanism designed to encourage long-term participation in DeFi protocols.

Users lock their tokens for a fixed period in exchange for governance power and additional rewards.

---

# How veToken Systems Work

Users deposit protocol tokens into a locking contract.

In return they receive a non-transferable voting token.

Example structure:

Lock tokens → receive veTokens → gain governance power.

The longer the lock period, the greater the voting power.

---

# Incentive Alignment

The veToken model aligns incentives between users and the protocol.

Participants who commit long-term capital receive:

- higher rewards
- stronger governance influence
- boosted liquidity incentives

Short-term participants receive fewer benefits.

---

# Liquidity Wars

Some ecosystems built around veTokens created competitive dynamics known as “liquidity wars”.

Protocols compete to attract governance votes that direct reward emissions toward their liquidity pools.

This dynamic can create complex strategic behavior between protocols.

---

# Advantages

The veToken model encourages:

- long-term token holding
- active governance participation
- deeper liquidity commitment

---

# Tradeoffs

Locking tokens reduces liquidity and flexibility for users.

If token prices decline significantly, locked holders may experience opportunity costs.

---

# Conclusion

The veToken model represents one of the most influential tokenomic designs in DeFi.

By rewarding long-term commitment, it attempts to balance liquidity incentives with sustainable protocol governance.
