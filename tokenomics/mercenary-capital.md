# Mercenary Capital in DeFi

Mercenary capital refers to liquidity that enters a protocol only to capture short-term rewards and exits once incentives decline.

This behavior is common in liquidity mining programs.

---

# Characteristics of Mercenary Capital

Mercenary capital typically:

- follows the highest yield opportunities
- moves rapidly between protocols
- has little long-term commitment

Because capital is highly mobile in DeFi, liquidity providers frequently shift funds to maximize returns.

---

# Effects on Protocols

Mercenary capital can create several challenges.

## Liquidity Instability

When incentives decline, large amounts of liquidity may leave the protocol quickly.

This can cause:

- reduced trading efficiency
- increased slippage
- volatile market conditions

---

## Token Price Pressure

Reward tokens earned by liquidity miners are often sold immediately.

This can create continuous selling pressure on the protocol token.

---

# Strategies to Reduce Mercenary Capital

Protocols attempt to discourage short-term behavior through several mechanisms.

Examples include:

- token lockups
- governance staking
- vote-escrow token models
- protocol-owned liquidity

These designs attempt to align incentives with long-term protocol growth.

---

# Long-Term Liquidity

Some protocols prioritize “sticky liquidity” — liquidity that remains even when incentives decline.

This often requires aligning economic incentives with long-term participation.

---

# Conclusion

Mercenary capital is a natural consequence of open financial markets.

Designing tokenomics that encourage long-term participation remains one of the key challenges in DeFi protocol design.
