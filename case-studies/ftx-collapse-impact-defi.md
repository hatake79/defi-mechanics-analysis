# FTX Collapse and Impact on DeFi

The collapse of the FTX exchange in November 2022 was one of the largest failures in the history of the cryptocurrency industry.

Although FTX was a centralized exchange, its failure had significant effects across the DeFi ecosystem.

---

# Background

FTX was one of the largest crypto exchanges globally.

It was closely connected with the trading firm Alameda Research.

Alameda used large amounts of leverage and had exposure to many crypto assets.

---

# Liquidity Crisis

Concerns about Alameda's balance sheet triggered massive withdrawals from FTX.

The exchange quickly became insolvent.

FTX halted withdrawals and later filed for bankruptcy.

---

# DeFi Impact

Although DeFi protocols are non-custodial, the collapse affected the ecosystem in several ways.

---

## Liquidity Shock

Many market makers and funds suffered losses.

This reduced liquidity across both centralized and decentralized markets.

---

## Token Price Declines

The collapse triggered a large market-wide selloff.

Many DeFi tokens lost significant value.

---

## Trust Shift Toward DeFi

Ironically, the event also reinforced the value of decentralized finance.

Because DeFi protocols are transparent and non-custodial, users began emphasizing self-custody.

---

# Lessons

Key lessons include:

Transparency and on-chain verification are critical advantages of DeFi.

Centralized risk can still influence decentralized ecosystems.

Risk management and diversification remain essential.
