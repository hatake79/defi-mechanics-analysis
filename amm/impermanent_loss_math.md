# Impermanent Loss

Impermanent loss occurs when token prices diverge after liquidity is deposited.

Liquidity providers effectively rebalance their portfolio automatically.

---

## Example

ETH price increases relative to USDC.

Arbitrage traders rebalance the pool.

Liquidity providers end up holding less ETH and more USDC.

---

## Result

The LP portfolio becomes less valuable compared to holding the assets outside the pool.
