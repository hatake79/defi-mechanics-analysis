# Constant Product AMM

The constant product model was popularized by Uniswap.

Formula:

x * y = k

Where:

x = reserve of token A  
y = reserve of token B  
k = constant product

---

## Price Mechanism

Price is determined by the ratio of reserves:

price = y / x

Trades move the reserves and therefore the price.

---

## Key Properties

- continuous liquidity
- predictable price impact
- simple design

---

## Limitations

Large trades create significant slippage.
