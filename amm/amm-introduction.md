# AMM Introduction

Automated Market Makers (AMMs) are the core infrastructure behind decentralized exchanges.

Instead of matching buyers and sellers through an order book, AMMs rely on liquidity pools and pricing algorithms.

Users trade against the liquidity pool rather than directly with another trader.

---

## Core Components

AMM systems include:

- liquidity pools
- liquidity providers
- pricing formulas
- arbitrage traders

Liquidity providers deposit assets into pools and earn trading fees.

---

## Advantages

AMMs enable:

- permissionless market creation
- continuous liquidity
- automated pricing

---

## Challenges

AMMs face several design challenges:

- impermanent loss
- price slippage
- MEV exploitation
