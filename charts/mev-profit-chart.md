# MEV Profit Distribution

This chart visualizes the distribution of MEV profits across different strategies.

MEV represents value extracted from transaction ordering within a block.

---

# Major MEV Categories

Common MEV strategies include:

DEX arbitrage

Traders exploit price differences between decentralized exchanges.

Sandwich attacks

Attackers insert transactions before and after large swaps.

Liquidations

Liquidators repay undercollateralized loans to capture discounted collateral.

---

# Chart Structure

The visualization would typically include:

x-axis: time

y-axis: cumulative MEV profit

stacked categories:

- arbitrage
- sandwich attacks
- liquidations

---

# Insights

Arbitrage strategies historically generate the largest share of MEV profits.

However, sandwich attacks represent one of the most controversial forms of MEV.

The emergence of MEV mitigation solutions may reshape the distribution of profits.
