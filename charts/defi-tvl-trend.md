# DeFi Total Value Locked (TVL) Trends

Total Value Locked (TVL) measures the amount of capital deposited in DeFi protocols.

TVL is commonly used as a proxy for ecosystem growth.

---

# TVL Growth Phases

Phase 1 — Early DeFi

TVL was below $1B before the DeFi summer of 2020.

Phase 2 — Rapid Expansion

TVL surged as lending, AMMs, and yield farming grew rapidly.

Phase 3 — Market Correction

Market downturns reduced TVL but usage remained strong.

---

# TVL by Sector

DeFi TVL can be broken down into several sectors.

Lending protocols  
Decentralized exchanges  
Derivatives platforms  
Stablecoin systems

---

# Key Insight

TVL alone does not fully represent protocol health.

Other important metrics include:

- trading volume
- fee generation
- active users
- liquidity efficiency
