# Lending Liquidations Analysis

This chart tracks liquidation events across major DeFi lending protocols.

Liquidations are critical to maintaining solvency in overcollateralized lending systems.

# Protocols Analyzed

Aave  
Compound

# Chart Structure

The visualization typically shows:

x-axis: time

y-axis: liquidation volume

Major spikes usually correspond with large market price drops.

---

# Observed Patterns

Liquidation spikes occur during periods of extreme market volatility.

Large price corrections can trigger cascading liquidations across multiple protocols.

---

# Risk Implications

Understanding liquidation dynamics helps evaluate systemic risk in DeFi lending markets.

Protocols must carefully calibrate collateral ratios and liquidation thresholds.
