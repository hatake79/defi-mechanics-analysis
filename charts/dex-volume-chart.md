# DEX Volume Chart

This chart analyzes the growth of decentralized exchange trading volume over time.

DEX trading volume is one of the most important indicators of DeFi adoption.

---

# Key Observations

Major growth phases in DEX trading volume:

2020 – Early DeFi Summer  
2021 – Rapid expansion of AMM platforms  
2022 – Market downturn but stable usage  
2023+ – Growth driven by layer 2 networks

---

# Drivers of DEX Volume

Several factors influence decentralized exchange activity.

Market volatility

Higher volatility generally increases trading activity.

Liquidity incentives

Liquidity mining campaigns can significantly increase trading volume.

Gas costs

Lower transaction fees on layer-2 networks encourage more frequent trading.
