# MEV Profit Breakdown

Maximal Extractable Value (MEV) represents the additional profit that can be extracted through transaction ordering.

This analysis explores how MEV profits are generated and distributed across the ecosystem.

---

# Major MEV Categories

MEV strategies typically fall into several categories.

DEX arbitrage

Traders exploit price differences between exchanges.

Liquidations

Liquidators repay debt in lending protocols to receive discounted collateral.

Sandwich attacks

Attackers exploit slippage in large trades.

---

# Profit Distribution

MEV profits are distributed among several participants.

Searchers

Identify and exploit opportunities.

Builders

Construct optimized blocks.

Validators

Select the most profitable block proposal.

---

# Revenue Flow

The MEV supply chain creates a competitive market for blockspace.

Searchers compete to submit bundles containing profitable transactions.

Builders assemble these bundles into blocks.

Validators choose the highest-paying block.

---

# Market Size

MEV markets have generated billions of dollars in cumulative profits since the emergence of decentralized finance.

However, MEV also raises concerns regarding fairness and market efficiency.

---

# Emerging Solutions

Several approaches aim to reduce negative MEV effects.

Examples include:

- batch auctions
- encrypted mempools
- intent-based trading

---

# Conclusion

Understanding MEV profit distribution is essential for designing fair and efficient blockchain markets.
