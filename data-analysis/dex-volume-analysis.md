# DEX Volume Analysis

This analysis explores trading volume patterns across decentralized exchanges.

DEX volume is one of the most important metrics for evaluating the health of DeFi markets.

---

# Key Metrics

Important indicators include:

Daily trading volume

Total value locked (TVL)

Liquidity depth

Trade size distribution

---

# Major DEX Categories

DEX platforms can be categorized based on their trading mechanisms.

AMM-based exchanges

Examples:

- Uniswap
- Curve
- Balancer

Orderbook-based exchanges

Examples:

- dYdX
- Injective

Batch auction systems

Examples:

- CoW Protocol

---

# Volume Drivers

Several factors influence DEX trading volume.

Market volatility

Higher volatility typically increases trading activity.

Liquidity incentives

Liquidity mining programs can significantly boost trading volume.

Cross-chain activity

Volume often migrates between chains depending on gas costs and incentives.

---

# Liquidity Efficiency

Capital efficiency is critical for AMM systems.

The traditional constant-product AMM follows the invariant:

x \cdot y = k

Where:

x = reserve of token A  
y = reserve of token B  
k = constant product

This formula determines how prices adjust after each trade.
