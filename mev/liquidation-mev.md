# Liquidation MEV

Liquidation opportunities represent one of the most profitable forms of MEV in DeFi lending markets.

When a borrower's collateral falls below a required threshold, their position becomes eligible for liquidation.

Liquidators compete to execute these transactions.

---

# Liquidation Incentives

Protocols offer a liquidation bonus to incentivize liquidators.

Example:

Collateral value = $10,000  
Borrowed value = $8,500  

Liquidator repays part of the debt and receives collateral at a discount.

This difference becomes profit.

---

# Liquidation Bots

Liquidation opportunities are extremely competitive.

Professional liquidators run automated bots that:

- monitor borrower health factors
- track price movements
- submit transactions instantly

These bots often integrate with MEV infrastructure to gain priority execution.

---

# Network Congestion

During market crashes, thousands of positions may become liquidatable simultaneously.

This creates intense competition for block space.

Only the fastest bots successfully capture liquidation profits.

---

# Interaction with MEV

Liquidation transactions often appear in MEV bundles submitted directly to block builders.

This ensures they are executed before competing transactions.

---

# Conclusion

Liquidation MEV plays an important role in maintaining the solvency of lending protocols while simultaneously creating a competitive market for automated trading bots.
