# Major DeFi Protocols

This document lists important DeFi protocols and their documentation.

---

# Decentralized Exchanges

## Uniswap

Leading automated market maker on Ethereum.

https://docs.uniswap.org/

Key concepts:
- constant product AMM
- concentrated liquidity
- liquidity provider fees

---

## Curve Finance

Specialized AMM for stablecoins.

https://curve.fi/

Key features:
- low slippage stable swaps
- efficient liquidity pools

---

## Balancer

Multi-asset liquidity pools.

https://docs.balancer.fi/

Key features:
- weighted pools
- programmable liquidity
